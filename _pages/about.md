Hi.

I'm Luoye, an assistant professor at the Carbon Neutrality and Climate Change Thrust (CNCC) and the Innovation, Policy, and Entrepreneurship (IPE) Thrust of The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (GZ). My research interests lie in the felds of environmental and natural resource economics and related interdisciplinary felds. In my research, I seek to understand how the interaction between economic and natural systems affects agricultural production, renewable resources, and human adaptation. I combine different theoretical and empirical strategies in my research, such as causal inference, machine learning, new sources of digital data (text and satellite images) and optimal control theories.
